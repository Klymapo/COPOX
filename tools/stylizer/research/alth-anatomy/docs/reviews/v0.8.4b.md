# ALTH_META v0.8.4b — Silhouette Refinement B

Candidate created after visual review of v0.8.4a. It remains a test candidate; prior versions are kept intact.

## Changes
- Head width/height kept at 40 × 34.5 mm, but effective depth reduced to about 29.8 mm.
- Lower jaw narrowed to 36.2 mm.
- Neck reduced to 9 × 8 × 4 mm and pushed further into the torso/head transition.
- Torso adjusted to 30.5 mm chest, 25 mm waist, 25.2 mm hip and 19.3 mm height.
- Pelvis reduced to 5.8 mm height to reduce the belt/block appearance.
- Shoulder/deltoid profile enlarged slightly and arm root moved further inside the torso.
- Palm increased to 7.5 mm long and 9.4 mm wide at finger base.
- Fingers enlarged slightly; thumb profile strengthened.
- Leg length remains 24 mm; knee/calf/ankle rhythm refined.
- Shoes remain 9 mm high but grow to 18 mm long, with the extra length concentrated toward the toe.
- Ears increased to 6.5 × 3.8 × 8.4 mm.
- Eye width/height adjusted to 8.4 × 9.7 mm.

## Still intentionally excluded
- Two-segment fingers.
- Regional mesh fusion.
- Hair.
- Clothing.
- Rigging.

## Review views requested
Front, 3/4, profile/side if possible, and low-angle view. Focus on head depth, torso/pelvis continuity, hand presence, calf rhythm, and toe-box length.
